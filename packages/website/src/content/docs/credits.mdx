Tegaki stands on other people's fonts and handwriting data. This page credits them and carries the notices their licenses ask to travel with them — the Studio runs the generator in your browser, so this site serves some of that data itself.

## Bundled fonts

The font bundles under `tegaki/fonts/*` are each under their font's own license: the SIL Open Font License for all but Hershey Script, which carries the Hershey Fonts' terms (below). Every font's designer, copyright and license is listed in [`FONTS-LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/gkurt/tegaki/blob/main/packages/renderer/FONTS-LICENSE.md), which ships in the npm package.

## Stroke-order datasets

The generator orders each glyph's strokes by the reference that fits its ink best, from these datasets (Studio › Pipeline › References):

- **[KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net)** — Japanese kanji and kana, also print Latin and digits. Copyright © Ulrich Apel, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Read per character from KanjiVG's files when a glyph needs it.
- **[Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi)** — Chinese hanzi in PRC order. Copyright © Shaunak Kishore; its graphics are derived from Arphic PL fonts and released under the Arphic Public License. Read per character from Hanzi Writer's republication, the [`hanzi-writer-data`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/hanzi-writer-data) package.
- **[The Hershey Fonts](https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts)** — Hershey Script, Simplex and Greek, built into the generator. Their acknowledgement is below.
- **[Letterpaths](https://github.com/RobinL/letterpaths)** — print letters and taught cursive lowercase, built into the generator. Its MIT notice is below.
- **Hangul jamo** and **Punctuation** — made for Tegaki, under its MIT license.

Datasets you draw in the Studio are yours; they stay in your browser.

## Single-line fonts in the Studio

The Studio's font picker lists single-line fonts, downloaded by your browser from their authors' repositories when you pick one:

- The Hershey and EMS fonts from Evil Mad Scientist's [SVG fonts](https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts), converted by Windell H. Oskay. The EMS fonts are under the SIL Open Font License; the Hershey fonts under the Hershey Fonts' terms below.
- [Relief SingleLine](https://github.com/isdat-type/Relief-SingleLine) by isdaT type, under the SIL Open Font License.
