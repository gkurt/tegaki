A collection of showcase videos rendered with Tegaki and [Remotion](/frameworks/remotion/).

## Tegaki 1.0

The launch video: every handwritten letter in it is drawn by Tegaki, in controlled time. It shows twelve scripts, one word through eight plugins written for it, the Studio, and a title scrubbed back and forth in a video editor. Its source, including the plugins and the soundtrack script, is in [`examples/remotion`](https://github.com/gkurt/tegaki/tree/main/examples/remotion/src/launch).
